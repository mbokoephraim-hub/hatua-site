By signing a memorandum of understanding, HATUA Foundation and Lycée Tobongisa are entering an educational collaboration that will benefit **262 eighth-grade girls**. The initiative aims to strengthen the support they receive, particularly as they approach the choice of their school tracks.

**Kinshasa, 9 November 2026.** HATUA Foundation and Lycée Tobongisa, located in the commune of Ngaliema, have taken a new step in their collaboration with the official signing of a memorandum of understanding. This partnership paves the way for the rollout of the ÉCHOS educational programme, the Choice Laboratory, scheduled to launch on 12 December.

<figure><img src="/images/articles/tobongisa/signature.webp" alt="A school official signs the ÉCHOS memorandum of understanding, a sheet of paper resting on the document" width="1600" height="1067" decoding="async"><figcaption>The signing of the memorandum of understanding at Lycée Tobongisa.</figcaption></figure>

The meeting took place at the school, in the presence of the school head, Sister Mulosi Mayamba Thérèse, who entrusted the follow-up of the partnership to Buhika Kutuna Godefroid, the school’s educational advisor. The foundation was represented by Sublime Koyi Saley, president and founder of HATUA Foundation, and Gustave-Ramon Kakala, coordinator of ÉCHOS activities at the foundation.

<figure><img src="/images/articles/tobongisa/signature-eleve.webp" alt="A HATUA Foundation representative, in a green T-shirt, leans over the desk to sign the memorandum of understanding" width="1600" height="1067" decoding="async"><figcaption>HATUA Foundation signs the memorandum in turn.</figcaption></figure>

## A collaboration serving students

Beyond the signing, this collaboration reflects a shared will: to better support students in the decisions that will shape the rest of their school path and their lives.

At Lycée Tobongisa, 262 eighth-grade girls are getting ready to choose their track among the literary, commercial, teaching, social and aesthetics sections. It is a step that raises many questions, both for the students and for those around them.

<figure><img src="/images/articles/tobongisa/poignee-de-main.webp" alt="A smiling handshake in a school office between a school official and a HATUA Foundation representative" width="1600" height="1067" decoding="async"><figcaption>A handshake sealing the shared commitment.</figcaption></figure>

To meet this need, HATUA Foundation and the school intend to combine their efforts to offer the girls a space for reflection and support that complements their regular lessons.

## A project warmly welcomed

During the discussions, Buhika Kutuna Godefroid, educational advisor, praised the initiative led by HATUA Foundation, calling it a very good project.

> “It is a very good project, because at this age, students need support to better know their aptitudes and make informed choices. This collaboration strengthens the educational work we carry out within our school.”

<figure><img src="/images/articles/tobongisa/declaration.webp" alt="In the school yard, a school official speaks into a lapel microphone next to a HATUA Foundation representative" width="1600" height="1067" decoding="async"><figcaption>In the school yard, after the signing.</figcaption></figure>

This warm welcome reflects the school’s interest in an approach that combines school education with students’ self-knowledge.

The collaboration will notably make it possible to organise educational meetings that encourage dialogue, self-knowledge and reflection around orientation decisions.

## A first date set for 12 December

The first concrete result of this partnership will be the official launch of ÉCHOS, the Choice Laboratory, on **Saturday 12 December 2026**.

Held under the theme “Who influences my choices?”, this first activity will bring together the 262 students concerned. It will open a programme that includes an interactive conference, two support sessions and an educational showcase.

At Tobongisa, the agreement is now signed. What remains is to bring this commitment to life every day, alongside these 262 young girls whose choices today will help shape tomorrow’s paths.
